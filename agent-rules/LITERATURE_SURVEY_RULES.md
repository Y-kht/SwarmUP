# No survey should be generated without strictly following these rules.

## Rules for the survey:
1) Only use the papers in the list provided. Never invent a paper, and never add one from memory.
2) Cite every paper that is used by its title and its exact link from the list. A paper without its link is not cited.
3) Only state what the title, the abstract or the summary of a paper says. Never claim a result, a method or a number that is not in them.
4) If a paper only has a short description, do not pretend that its full text was read.
5) Group the papers by theme, and say where the papers agree and where they disagree.
6) Do not cite the same paper twice.
7) Respect the subject the user asked for and the maximum number of words.
8) Keep a neutral, scholarly tone. Do not give personal opinions.
9) The survey is only saved after the user's explicit approval.
